antares-biblia

antares-biblia is a personal, locally-run tool used by its owner to manage the YouTube channel Cenas da Bíblia no Dia a Dia: uploading and scheduling the owner's own videos, editing their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and reading the owner's own channel metrics to plan new content.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no user accounts other than the channel owner's own Google account (OAuth 2.0).

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
